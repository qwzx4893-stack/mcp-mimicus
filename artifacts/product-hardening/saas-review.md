## Fixed task and equal inspection

Relay is an async decision log for small distributed product teams replacing scattered chat decisions. Both variants were judged against the same requested content and behavior: a 14-day local demonstration, introduction, decision log, features, two plans with monthly/annual billing, FAQ, three category filters and mobile navigation with Escape/focus return. The prescribed system type and white/slate/cobalt palette were common to both.

Each alias received the same sequence: open at **1440×900**, inspect semantic controls, open the header trial dialog, Escape, select Design, select Annual, open the first FAQ, resize to **390×844 with reduced motion**, scroll to the opening, open mobile navigation, then Escape. Actual MCP images were viewed at the meaningful states. No code inspection, source discovery or outside reference was used for this review. Twenty-two calls performed the matched inspection; two additional calls closed both sessions and checked resource status. The client was then closed. No site image was saved to disk.

Both variants visibly reacted to the tested controls without a tool error. Trial-dialog completion beyond its initial state was not tested equally: B contained a team-name form, while A offered a direct demo-workspace button. This review therefore does not certify full trial submission, keyboard focus trapping, all categories, every FAQ, exact pricing arithmetic, every narrow width or screen-reader behavior. Those limits affect the behavior score equally.

## Specific outcomes

- **Trial dialog:** A showed “Make room for your next decision,” an Open demo workspace action and explicit local/no-account-data copy. B showed “Your team, on the same page,” a Team name field and Preview trial, also explicitly local/no account. Escape returned to the page in each case.
- **Decision filter:** A's Design selection changed the detail to “Use plain language for decision states.” B's Design selection left one example decision, “Make progress visible at every step,” and a visible one-example count. The interaction models differ but both communicate state.
- **Pricing:** A annual displayed Team $8 and Studio $16 per member/month, with annual totals/billing language. B annual displayed Starter $8 and Team $16 per person/month with billed-annually language. Different plan names are original content choices; this review did not treat them as an inconsistency across separate variants.
- **FAQ:** Both opened the first answer and explained that Relay complements chat rather than replacing it.
- **Mobile menu:** Both displayed links in an overlay panel and closed on Escape. Screenshots showed a visible focus ring returned to Menu. The panel temporarily covers hero content while open, an expected navigation state.

## Conclusion and cleanup

This narrow matched review found **different strengths and no clear overall winner**. It cannot support a claim that assistance improved aesthetic quality, conversion or task performance.
