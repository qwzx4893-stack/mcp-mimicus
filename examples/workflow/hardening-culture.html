A room for
another thought.

Notes from the exhibition. Open a story and stay a while.

The doorstep as a meeting placeEssay · 4 minute read

A doorway marks the edge of a room, but it can also make a space to pause. A bench, a patch of shade or a wide step can turn passing neighbours into familiar faces. The models in this exhibition ask what happens when the threshold becomes part of the shared life of a street.

Drawing a home that can changeStudio notes · 3 minute read

Homes hold many lives over time. A room that begins as a workshop may become a bedroom or a place for a parent to stay. These speculative plans leave generous openings and movable partitions so that a building can adapt without starting again.

Small gardens, shared futuresConversation · 5 minute read

The courtyard is both an outdoor room and a common responsibility. Here, residents make decisions about shade, water and planting together. The question is not only how a garden looks, but who has the time and opportunity to care for it.
