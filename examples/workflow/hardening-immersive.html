Nothing stays at the edge.

A shoreline is a place of quiet exchange. This small digital installation gives that movement a shape, with light that settles into each rising surface.

Field notes & ways to explore

Turn the scene by dragging, tapping the water, or focusing it and using the arrow keys. Choose a light and a water state, then move the tide-height control.

You can pause the tide at any time. If your device asks for less motion, the water stays still while every control remains available. No sound, account, or download is needed.
