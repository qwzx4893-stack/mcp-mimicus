A few things
worth knowing.

Make a little room for better decisions. Start with the team you already have.

Does Relay replace our team chat?

No. Chat is where conversations happen. Relay is where you keep the outcome, the reasoning and the owner so people can find them later.

Can we try it without a credit card?

Yes. The 14-day trial needs no card. In this local demonstration, the trial button opens an original demo workspace without sending data anywhere.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed once per year at a 20% lower equivalent monthly rate. The pricing switch shows both options clearly. No payment is processed in this demo.

What should we record first?

Start with a recent product choice someone had to ask about twice. Add the decision, a short explanation and the person who can give more context.
