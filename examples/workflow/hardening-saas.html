A few good questions.

Here’s how this Relay concept works.

What happens when I start the trial?

This is a local demo. The button opens a sample trial preview, then takes you to the decision log. No account is created, no email is collected, and no trial timer or payment starts.

Does Relay replace our team chat?

The concept is designed to sit beside chat. Discuss wherever you work, then capture the final decision and its reasoning in Relay so it can be found later.

Can teammates contribute asynchronously?

In the proposed product, teammates could add context, review options, and read the final decision on their own schedule. This demo illustrates the log and category filters only.

Can I change plans or cancel?

The fictional plans are shown to help you compare scope. There is no active subscription to change or cancel, and nothing on this page connects to a billing service.
